01 / About

I like making systems work in the real world.

My work spans digital hardware, board bring-up, test engineering, and software automation. I enjoy the point where a design stops being theoretical and has to work on actual hardware.

At Nokia, I’ve worked across FPGA verification, serial interfaces, hardware validation, optical and electrical test setups, and automated factory testing. At Lehigh, I’ve combined hardware design with research, teaching, and a senior capstone focused on real-time image processing on an FPGA.

I’m especially interested in roles involving FPGA development, embedded systems, hardware test, systems engineering, or low-level software close to the hardware.

Outside of engineering, I play volleyball, spend time with friends, hike, and travel. I also enjoy meeting people and building with new teammates at hackathons.
