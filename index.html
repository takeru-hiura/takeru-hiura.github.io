02 / Experience

Where I've worked.

Photonic Test Engineer Co-op @ Nokia Bell Labs

Aug 2026 – Present · Murray Hill, New Jersey

I spend my days building DC power filter test benches, wiring up programmable supplies, load boards, and ADC measurement so the lab can validate hardware reliably and repeatably. A lot of that comes down to writing Python tooling that turns manual bench work into automated test cases anyone on the team can rerun. When something behaves unexpectedly, I dig in with oscilloscopes and optical, RF, and electrical instrumentation until I understand what the hardware is actually doing.

H

Data Analyst Intern @ Hyphenova

Aug 2026 – Present · Remote

My focus here is making data trustworthy before it reaches anyone downstream. I build and test pipelines that scrape, clean, and validate incoming data, then shape it into workflows the rest of the application can rely on. On the database side, I've helped design schemas, sort out messy legacy data, and document how everything connects through ERDs.

Hardware Development Co-op @ Nokia Bell Labs

Jan 2026 – Aug 2026 · Murray Hill, New Jersey

This co-op was about giving engineers better tools to trust their hardware. I built FPGA verification and debug infrastructure in Verilog and VHDL, paired with Python automation and embedded logic analyzers, so issues surfaced faster and earlier. Across more than 100 optical and telecom modules and 20-plus validation procedures, I tracked thermal, signal, and power behavior, while also handling board bring-up, firmware integration, and interface debugging directly on the bench.

L

Group Tutor @ Lehigh University

Aug 2025 – Dec 2025 · Bethlehem, Pennsylvania

I worked with more than 80 students to make digital logic click, from Boolean algebra and sequential circuits to microcomputer architecture. Rather than just walking through solutions, I tried to build their intuition for digital circuit design and analysis, plus the assembly-level programming that ties it all together.
